The studio

I am Ada Sample, and that is a sample name: this page shows where your own story goes.

I throw stoneware on the wheel, glaze it in a single blue, and fire it in small batches. Every piece is made to be used every day, and to go in the dishwasher.

How a piece is made

  1. Thrown

    On the wheel, from a speckled stoneware clay.

  2. Glazed

    Dipped or brushed, with one cobalt glaze mixed in the studio.

  3. Fired

    Twice, the second time at 1,250 °C.
